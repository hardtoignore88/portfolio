IT Forms

Research, Heuristic Evaluation, User Testing, UX Design, UI Design, Wireframes, Prototyping

IT forms on LBG IT Intranet showed recurring usability problems around content, navigation, interaction, layout and visual design, including unclear terminology, inconsistent form behaviours, weak validation and inappropriate eCommerce patterns.

The Plan

Create a structured way to identify and categorise these problems, recommend improvements, and translate the findings into reusable guidance for designing better IT forms.

The Process

I conducted a heuristic evaluation of journeys to find user pain points, performed research with user testing (moderated & unmoderated), documented recommendations, benchmarked 18+ external standards and toolkits, and distilled the findings into eight Good Form principles.

The principles were then applied to an end-to-end concept to demonstrate how the recommendations could work in practice.

  • Navigation & Usability
  • Interaction
  • Aesthetics
  • Content
  • Layout

Result

The work transformed individual UX pain points into a structured evidence base, a set of reusable form-design principles and a repeatable approach for defining, designing, testing, publishing and improving IT forms.

This was an exercise not only in improving the forms usability and intuitiveness but also an education for users who create the forms in terms of best practice and consistency.
